Suggested description for the team to review:

> Search RA and declination to play the infrared observations SPHEREx took of one fixed sky field. The tool keeps the dates, passbands and quality masks visible while browsing, blinking or comparing exposures. Six cached views cover NASA’s five named fields and both the historical and observation-era WISE 0855 coordinates. Optional overlays and spectra retain their catalog or calibration provenance. Motion estimates appear only when independent observations support the fit.
